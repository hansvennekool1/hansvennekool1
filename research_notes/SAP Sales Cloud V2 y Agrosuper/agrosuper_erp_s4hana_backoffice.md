## 1. Which ERP did Agrosuper use before, since when, and what motivated the move?

### Takeaway
Agrosuper has run SAP since 2005. Until at least September 2023 most of its operations ran on SAP ECC, which was heavily customised and used industry solutions such as SAP Meat and Fish Management. Agrosuper's own stated reasons for moving were the complexity of that customised ECC estate, the need for near-real-time data for decisions, and avoiding any production stoppage during the migration. SAP's end of mainstream ECC support (2027) is the general industry backdrop.

### Cited Findings
- Agrosuper chose SAP solutions in 2005 to support its business **[PARTNER/VENDOR]**: [Novis case study, "Agrosuper optimiza calidad de desarrollos en SAP para asegurar continuidad operacional"](https://noviscorp.com/es/sap/agrosuper-optimiza-calidad-de-desarrollos-en-sap-para-asegurar-continuidad-operacional/).
- In September 2023 Adolfo Moreno, Subgerente de Digital Factory at Agrosuper, said a large share of operational processes still ran on SAP ECC. He called the landscape "una implementación muy compleja en cuanto a los módulos y soluciones de industria", with custom developments and business logic, and said the migration carries risks that must be reduced because a food company "no puede arriesgarse a una paralización temporal de sus operaciones" **[CONFIRMED-primary]**: [SAP News Latinoamérica, Sep 2023, "Implementación tecnológica para la toma de decisiones en Agrosuper"](https://news.sap.com/latinamerica/2023/09/implementacion-tecnologica-para-toma-decisiones-en-agrosuper/); also republished on the [Agrosuper Investors site](https://www.agrosuper.com/inversionistas/chilena-agrosuper-implementa-tecnologia-para-tomar-decisiones-con-datos-en-tiempo-real/) and by [Tabulado](https://www.tabulado.net/agrosuper-acelera-su-transformacion-digital-con-nuevas-herramientas-para-la-toma-de-decisiones/).
- Industry solution in use: Agrosuper integrated **SAP Meat and Fish Management** in its plants to centralise data management, cut costs, trace product origin precisely and record plant data in real time. The company makes more than 1,600 pork, poultry, salmon and processed-meat products. - Earlier ECC-era projects by Novis **[PARTNER/VENDOR]**:
  - SAP QM rolled out in 2016 for inspection, planning and quality control across procurement, production and dispatch. It started at Planta Rosario, was then replicated to the other slaughter plants, and was meant to go paperless: [Novis, "Agrosuper optimiza el proceso de calidad en plantas con SAP Fiori"](https://www.noviscorp.com/es/sap/agrosuper-optimiza-el-proceso-de-calidad-en-plantas-con-sap-fiori/).
  - SAP Fiori installed, with HR users live in June 2016: [Novis, "Usuarios de Agrosuper dan muy buena recepción a Fiori"](https://www.noviscorp.com/es/sap/usuarios-de-agrosuper-dan-muy-buena-recepcion-a-fiori/).
  - SAP Solution Manager 7.2 for IT processes: [Novis, "Agrosuper implementa SolMan 7.2…"](https://www.novis.cl/noticias-novis/industria/agrosuper-implementa-solman-7-2-para-optimizar-procesos-del-area-ti/).
- Infrastructure under SAP: in November 2017 Agrosuper was a beta participant in Pure Storage's Copy Automation Tool for SAP. It reportedly copied and cloned a 21 TB SAP database in 20 minutes, against a typical 9 days **[PARTNER/VENDOR]**: [StorageNewsletter, Pure Storage CAT for SAP](https://www.storagenewsletter.com/?p=183165); [ITWare Latam, 10 Oct 2017, "Agrosuper agiliza sus sistemas SAP con Pure Storage"](https://www.itwarelatam.com/2017/10/10/agrosuper-agiliza-sus-sistemas-sap-con-pure-storage/).

Search metadata suggests the article is from the early 2000s, so it is relevant only as history **[PARTNER/VENDOR]**: [Revista Gerencia, "Agrosuper: Actualiza su plataforma tecnológica con S&A Chile"](https://www.gerencia.cl/proveedores/agrosuper-actualiza-su-plataforma-tecnologica-con-sa-chile/).
- General backdrop, not Agrosuper-specific: standard ECC support ends 31 Dec 2027, according to a partner blog: [Quality Group blog](https://quality.global/blog/migracion-sap-s-4hana-guia-transformacion-erp).

### Inferences
- The ECC landscape was mature and heavily customised: industry solution (Meat and Fish Management), QM, HR with Fiori, SolMan, and a SAP database of more than 21 TB on Pure Storage flash. That makes a single big-bang greenfield unlikely and fits the "gradual / progressive" migration language SAP used in 2023.
- The 2027 end of ECC mainstream maintenance probably added time pressure, but no Agrosuper source says so.

### Gaps
- No source says whether Agrosuper ran R/3 before ECC, or which ECC enhancement pack and database it used. Pure Storage hints at a large Oracle or HANA database, but I could not confirm which.
- Agrosuper has not publicly stated end-of-maintenance as a motivation.

## 2. Did Agrosuper migrate to SAP S/4HANA? Announcement, partners, approach, RISE, hosting, go-live, completed by 2026?

### Takeaway
Yes, with medium-high confidence. In July 2026 SAP's Latin America newsroom published a customer story saying Agrosuper **"implementó SAP Cloud ERP Private"**, SAP's 2025 name for RISE with SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition. Indirect evidence supports it: a job posting asking for "S/4HANA RISE" skills, a "Gerente de proyectos SAP S/4 HANA Corporativo" role, and consultants describing a 2025 ECC-to-S/4 project and its go-live. No public source gives the go-live date, the system integrator, the hyperscaler or data centre, the migration approach (brownfield, greenfield or selective), the modules or companies in scope, or AquaChile's inclusion.

### Cited Findings

- "Implementó SAP Cloud ERP Private" in SAP's own newsroom, together with a posting asking for "S/4HANA RISE", strongly suggests Agrosuper signed RISE with SAP and runs S/4HANA in SAP's private cloud: SAP-managed, on SAP's or a hyperscaler's infrastructure. The hosting provider is not public.

## 4. What changed in planning, logistics, supply chain, finance and information management? Measurable results

### Takeaway
Documented changes are mostly from the satellite projects. SAP IBP is used for finished-goods planning, with a monthly cycle and standardised data. Datasphere and SAC give near-real-time ERP data for plant decisions. SAP's 2026 story claims budgeting processes went from hours to minutes. Treasury was centralised with cash pooling. AI-based stock allocation is claimed to cut 90% of allocation time and capture US$3.6M. No ERP-specific results (financial close, inventory, service level) were found.

### Cited Findings
- **Planning (SAP IBP):** Agrosuper implemented SAP IBP to plan and manage finished products. It describes itself as a pioneer in Chile with a then-new tool, and says it gained demand visibility and traceability within a defined monthly cycle and standardised processes and data sources. This is critical because much of its product is sold fresh and "la salida debe ser sumamente rápida" **[CONFIRMED-primary]**: [SAP News Latinoamérica, May 2021](https://news.sap.com/latinamerica/2021/05/cadena-de-suministro-supply-chain-agrosuper/).
- **Information management (Datasphere and SAC):** both give "mejor disponibilidad de datos de su sistema SAP ERP, permitiendo el acceso a esta información desde otras aplicaciones externas en un tiempo casi inmediato". The pilot ran for 2-3 months in one stage of the industrial plants with Lion Consulting and Swap Consulting. After positive results, a second phase was set up to "mejorar, robustecer y definir bien la arquitectura" so the tool can later be extended to the whole value chain **[CONFIRMED-primary]**: [SAP News Latinoamérica, Sep 2023](https://news.sap.com/latinamerica/2023/09/implementacion-tecnologica-para-toma-decisiones-en-agrosuper/).
- **Finance and planning, 2026:** "La compañía redujo de horas a minutos la ejecución de sus procesos presupuestarios y avanzó hacia una gestión basada en datos más ágil y eficiente". The text appears in the Tabulado republication of SAP's July 2026 story; I could not confirm it appears in SAP's original **[CONFIRMED-primary via republication]**: [Tabulado](https://www.tabulado.net/agrosuper-potencia-su-transformacion-digital-con-sap-cloud-erp-private/). - **Change management:** "la incorporación de la gestión del cambio como componente estructural de cada proyecto". Specialised teams were set up in each business unit, and pilots were used as a validation mechanism before wider adoption **[CONFIRMED-primary]**: [SAP News Latinoamérica, Jul 2026](https://news.sap.com/latinamerica/2026/07/agrosuper-potencia-su-transformacion-digital-con-sap-cloud-erp-private/); [Tabulado](https://www.tabulado.net/agrosuper-potencia-su-transformacion-digital-con-sap-cloud-erp-private/).

Javier Ayala: "Antes nos demorábamos muchas horas en procesar órdenes de compra para asignar productos a nuestros clientes internacionales". A Chile stock-centralisation project uses AI to allocate product to each branch by demand and available stock, with "una captura de US$ 3,6 millones por eficiencias en gastos y capital de trabajo". The Digital Transformation area dates from 2022 **[CONFIRMED, company press release republished]**: [Radio Agricultura, 20 Nov 2025](https://www.radioagricultura.cl/noticias/nacional/transformacion-digital-de-agrosuper-genera-impacto-en-el-negocio-con-nuevas-herramientas-para-la-toma-de-decisiones_20251120/); [Portal Agro Chile, 2 Dec 2025](https://www.portalagrochile.cl/2025/12/02/transformacion-digital-de-agrosuper-genera-impacto-en-el-negocio-con-nuevas-herramientas-para-la-toma-de-decisiones/); [Supermercado al Día](https://supermercadoaldia.cl/2025/12/02/transformacion-digital-de-agrosuper-genera-impacto-en-elnegocio-con-nuevas-herramientas-para-la-toma-de-decisiones/).
