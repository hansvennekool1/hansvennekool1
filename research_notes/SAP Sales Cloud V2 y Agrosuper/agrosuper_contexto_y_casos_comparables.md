## 1. Agrosuper key figures (2024, 2025 and 1H2026): size, segments, export mix, channels, customers, logistics footprint, international presence

### Takeaway
Agrosuper is a vertically integrated Chilean protein group. In 2025 it had USD 4,649 M in revenue, up 8.7%, and USD 905 M in EBITDA excluding fair value, up 38.4%, across a Meat segment (pork, poultry, turkey, processed) and an Aquaculture (salmon) segment. Exports are about 58–63% of quarterly revenue. In Chile it serves a very large, fragmented customer base: about 72,600 clients in total, about 40,000 stores a month and more than 55,000 traditional-channel clients. It does this through 26 distribution centers or commercial offices and 710 trucks, plus 12 international offices. That scale and fragmentation are exactly the conditions in which ERP, CRM and analytics integration pays off.

### Inferences
- The domestic go-to-market mixes key accounts (supermarket chains, where the top 20 clients make up 27% of revenue) with a long tail (more than 55,000 traditional-channel clients, about 40,000 stores a month, 26 commercial offices or DCs, 710 trucks). This is the classic case for CRM (account, visit and territory management), B2B e-commerce and route-to-market analytics sitting on a single ERP order-to-cash backbone. (Inference from the cited figures.)
- 58–63% of revenue comes from exports, sold through 12 international offices. International order allocation and stock assignment are therefore significant processes; the company's AI "Auto-Allocation" project targets exactly this (Section 3).
- Leverage is low (Moody's upgrade; net debt/EBITDA of about 0.4x) and 2025 investment rose to USD 265 M. Together these suggest financial room for multi-year technology programs. This is an inference; no technology CAPEX figure was found.

## 2. What Agrosuper's official reports, IR pages and corporate sites say about digital transformation, technology, SAP, CRM, e-commerce, data, analytics, AI, automation and cybersecurity (2021–2026)

### Takeaway
Since at least the 2021 Reporte Integrado, Agrosuper's official materials have treated digital transformation, automation, data use and cybersecurity as strategic or material topics. A corporate Transformación Digital page reports training KPIs (1,333 employees trained and 45,409 hours in 2025). The company has a long SAP history: SAP ECC with custom "Z" developments, SAP PI, SAP CRM, SAP IBP from 2021, and SAP Datasphere plus SAP Analytics Cloud from 2023. The latest step is "SAP Cloud ERP Private", announced by SAP on 1 July 2026, which "began with the development of the B2B digital sales channel". No technology CAPEX amount and no named overall program (apart from "Ruta de la Innovación y Transformación Digital") was found in official reports.

## 5. Comparable cases: ERP (S/4HANA), CRM (SAP Sales Cloud or other) and analytics in large food and protein companies

### Takeaway
There are well-documented food and protein S/4HANA cases with results: Clemens Food Group (US pork), BRF/MBRF (Brazil protein), Colombina (Colombia food) and Ariztía (Chilean poultry, Agrosuper's direct competitor). Public SAP Sales Cloud V2 cases in food with quantified results are scarce. The only food-sector V2 example found is a Swiss distributor, cited by its partner. Most results are vendor-reported, and several projects (MBRF) have not yet published quantified benefits.

### Cited Findings

**Case A: Ariztía (Chile, poultry; Agrosuper's closest domestic peer)**
- [CONFIRMED-VENDOR] Ariztía implemented **SAP S/4HANA plus PO in 2022 for the commercial area only**, while "todo lo referente al mundo avícola y a las plantas productivas sigue bajo sistemas legados". It later upgraded from S/4HANA 2019 to **S/4HANA 2023 on AWS** with Novis to extend SAP support to 2030 and continue with the program's next phases. The upgrade "se completó sin afectar la logística de **200 camiones y 45.000 clientes activos**". — [Novis case](https://www.noviscorp.com/es/sap/ariztia-realiza-upgrade-a-sap-s-4hana-2023-en-aws-con-novis/)
- Lesson: a **commercial-first S/4HANA scope**, with production left on legacy systems, is a model already used in Chilean poultry. It mirrors Agrosuper's approach of starting from the B2B sales channel.

**Case B: BRF, now MBRF after merging with Marfrig (Brazil, poultry and pork)**
- [CONFIRMED-PRESS] BRF bought **RISE with SAP** and committed to a **clean-core** strategy. — [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/especial/sap/primeiro-cliente-sap-no-brasil-a-brf-adota-rise-with-sap/)

  - Early post-go-live initiatives are in finance, using BTP, iCore and Joule (SAP's AI copilot).
  - Sources: [Baguete – BRF](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/brf-conclui-migracao-para-s4-hana); [Baguete – MBRF](https://www.baguete.com.br/public/noticias/mbrf-conclui-migracao-para-s4-hana); context: [IT Forum](https://itforum.com.br/noticias/sap-s4hana-deadline/)

### Inferences
- Across the cases the pattern repeats. First the ERP core is modernized through RISE or Cloud ERP Private, often big-bang or brownfield. The documented value comes from standardization, real-time margin visibility, IT capacity freed up, and delivery and service. - Ariztía's commercial-first S/4HANA scope and Colombina's emphasis on service to 750,000 customers are the closest analogues to Agrosuper's own B2B-first approach and its fragmented traditional channel.
- Sales Cloud V2 is recent (generally available around 2023) and has few public references in large food companies, so Agrosuper would be an early large-protein adopter if it chose V2. This is an inference from the scarce evidence, not a confirmed market statistic.

## 6. General evidence on why food and CPG companies integrate CRM with ERP (order-to-cash, trade promotion, route-to-market, demand planning)

### Takeaway
The integration case rests on three things: sales users need ERP data (availability, pricing, order status) inside CRM; an order captured in CRM or e-commerce must flow straight into ERP order-to-cash; and trade-promotion and route-to-market decisions need joined-up data. Independent analyst evidence (McKinsey) supports the digital-B2B and traditional-trade coverage argument. Most quantified ERP–CRM or ERP–TPM integration benefits come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED-VENDOR] SAP says Sales Cloud "connects with SAP ERP and SAP S/4HANA", giving reps access to accounts, **product availability, pricing, quotes, orders and order status** without switching systems. The Portuguese features page cites native S/4HANA integration for **billing, pricing and availability checks**. — [SAP Sales Cloud](https://www.sap.com/products/crm/sales-cloud.html); [SAP Sales Cloud features (PT)](https://www.sap.com/portugal/products/crm/sales-cloud/features.html)
- [CONFIRMED-VENDOR/PRACTITIONER] Sales Cloud shows **available-to-promise (ATP)** while a quote or order is being created — [Knack Systems](https://www.knacksystems.com/sap-c4hana/sap-sales-cloud). In the common integration pattern, a confirmed quote or order in C4C automatically creates an SD sales order in ERP for fulfillment, delivery and billing — [ERPVITS](https://www.erpvits.com/blog/sap-c4c-integration-architecture-methods-and-best-practices/); V2-specific opportunity-to-order flow: [SAP Community](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-members/sap-sales-cloud-v2-amp-s-4hana-streamlining-opportunity-management-amp/ba-p/14053418).
- [CONFIRMED-ANALYST] McKinsey on fragmented retail finds CPG sales coverage badly skewed: the **largest outlets receive 80 visits a week from CPGs** while many others are underserved, and COVID-19 made store owners more open to digital ordering. — [McKinsey, "Digital disruption: The rise of eB2B in fragmented retail"](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/digital-disruption-the-rise-of-eb2b-in-fragmented-retail)
- [CONFIRMED-ANALYST] McKinsey on emerging Asia: where distributors share outlet-level data unevenly, "it [is] hard, if not impossible, for companies to produce clear, real-time insights on market trends". B2B platforms that aggregate small-shop orders lower the cost to serve. — [McKinsey CPG PDF](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/McKinsey/Industries/Consumer%20Packaged%20Goods/Our%20Insights/Staying%20ahead%20of%20the%20B2B%20ecosystem%20disruption%20in%20emerging%20Asia/Staying-ahead-of-the-B2B-ecosystem-disruption-in-emerging-Asia-v3.pdf)
- [CONFIRMED-ANALYST] McKinsey's 2026 Global B2B Pulse (about 4,000 decision-makers, 13 countries): **71% of B2B companies now offer e-commerce**, and among those **roughly one-third of revenue flows through digital channels**. — [McKinsey, June 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/marketing%20and%20sales/our%20insights/the%20surprising%20economics%20of%20b2b%20growth%20the%20new%20survival%20threshold%20and%20what%20it%20takes%20to%20thrive/the-surprising-economics-of-b2b-growth-the-new-survival-threshold-and-what-it-takes-to-thrive.pdf)
- [CONFIRMED-PRESS citing McKinsey] In the 2024 B2B Pulse, **73% of B2B buyers (up from 59%) will spend more than US$50,000 per online order**, and **39% (up from 28%) more than US$500,000**. — [Digital Commerce 360, Sept 2024](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2024/09/12/more-b2b-buyers-are-willing-to-spend-big-bucks-per-online-order/); see also [McKinsey, omnichannel in B2B](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/omnichannel-in-b2b-sales-the-new-normal-in-a-year-that-has-been-anything-but)
- [CONFIRMED-TRADE PRESS] IndustryWeek: when orders and invoices sit in one system they can be linked to promotion claims automatically, which speeds reimbursement and reduces retailer reconciliation. Integrated ERP keeps prices and discounts accurate on orders, which cuts deductions. — [IndustryWeek, "Better Together: ERP and Trade Promotions Management"](https://www.industryweek.com/technology-and-iiot/information-technology/article/21965711/better-together-erp-and-trade-promotions-management)
- [CONFIRMED-VENDOR] Infosys lists faster order-to-cash cycles and larger order sizes among the outcomes of well-managed, integrated trade promotions. — [Infosys TPM paper](https://www.infosys.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/Documents/trade-promotion-management.pdf)
- [UNVERIFIED, secondhand] Forrester is quoted as saying "the more TPM is integrated with other enterprise systems, the better". — [SDCExec](https://www.sdcexec.com/software-technology/article/12091095/want-to-maximize-your-return-on-investment-on-promotions)
- [CONFIRMED-PRESS, Agrosuper-specific illustration] Agrosuper's own figures show the mechanism at work. By 2023 digital B2B, web plus WhatsApp, was **18% of sales** to the Food Service and traditional channels, and an AI model (MAI) prioritizes rep visits across **more than 55,000 traditional-channel clients**. In both cases CRM-type and analytics capabilities depend on ERP billing and history data. — [Diario de la Carne, May 2024](https://www.diariodelacarne.cl/noticia/economia-y-mercados/2024/05/agrosuper-crece-un-35-en-su-venta-digital-a-traves-de-diferentes-canales); [Agrosuper.cl MAI](https://www.agrosuper.cl/agrosuper-optimiza-sus-ventas-en-canal-tradicional-con-innovadora-herramienta-de-inteligencia-artificial/)

### Inferences
- For Agrosuper the interlock works like this:
  - **ERP (S/4HANA / Cloud ERP Private)** is the order-to-cash, pricing, ATP and billing backbone across 26 commercial offices or DCs and 12 international offices.
  - **CRM (Sales Cloud)** handles account, visit and territory management for more than 55,000 traditional-channel clients, plus key-account management for supermarkets.
  - **B2B e-commerce and WhatsApp** handle self-service order capture.
  - **Analytics and AI** (Datasphere and SAC, MAI, Auto-Allocation) use the integrated data for visit prioritization, allocation and planning (IBP).
  - This is a synthesis of the cited sources and is not stated by Agrosuper.
- The McKinsey evidence on uneven outlet coverage (80 visits a week to the largest outlets, many others underserved) maps directly onto Agrosuper's MAI stated goal of reducing "territorial gaps".

### Gaps
- No public Gartner or Deloitte research on CRM–ERP integration in food or CPG was retrieved. Only vendor and trade-press material and McKinsey were found.
- No independent, quantified benchmark of CRM–ERP integration benefits specific to meat or protein companies was found.
