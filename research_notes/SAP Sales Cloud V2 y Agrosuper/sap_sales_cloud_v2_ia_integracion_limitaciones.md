## 1. AI capabilities: what is GA, beta or roadmap; licensing; data and region requirements

### Takeaway
SSCV2 has a real but uneven AI layer as of Oct 2026. GA items are Joule conversational assistance (Q&A, opportunity and account summaries, email drafting, record updates), ML scoring (deal intelligence win score, lead intelligence), meeting briefs, and Joule Studio for custom agents (GA Q1 2026, partner report). The packaged "Joule Assistants/Agents for sales" (Sales Assistant, Deal Qualification, Deal Closing) have shifting dates: Sapphire (May 2026) said Q2/Q3 2026, and SAP Connect (Oct 2026) pushed new CX assistants to Q1 2027. Licensing is split between "Base" AI included in subscriptions and "Premium" AI that consumes AI Units (SKU 8019164). The rules for which SSCV2 feature falls in which tier are only partly public.

### Inferences
- "AI in SSCV2" falls into three tiers of maturity: (a) GA ML scoring and Joule Q&A/summaries/drafting; (b) Joule Studio for build-your-own agents (GA 2026); (c) packaged, cross-object Joule agents for selling, where most announced items had not reached GA by Oct 2026 and have slipped at least once (Q2/Q3 2026 to Q1 2027). Buyers should contract only on items marked GA in the SAP Discovery Center or Roadmap Explorer on the signing date.
- The Base/Premium split means AI total cost depends on which features are used. Meeting briefs are Base; account synopsis is Premium. Get a written feature-to-tier mapping.
- For a Latin American tenant, data residency for Joule (cross-region processing) needs explicit confirmation. No Latin American Joule data center appeared in the extracted list.

## 2. Analytics, dashboards and forecasting; connection to SAP Analytics Cloud, Datasphere and Business Data Cloud

### Takeaway
SSCV2 reporting runs on an **embedded edition of SAP Analytics Cloud (eSAC)**, which is narrower than standalone SAC, plus native pipeline and forecast tools (Pipeline Manager, Forecast Tracker, home-page KPIs). Taking V2 data out to standalone SAC, Datasphere or BDC is not plug-and-play. A community practitioner reports that V2 exposes no OData endpoints for the SAC OData connector and that SAP's recommended route is Datasphere plus Integration Suite replication. Partners and users describe reporting as less flexible than Salesforce's.

### Cited Findings
- SAP Learning: SSCV2 "creates comprehensive reports and interactive dashboards using the power of embedded SAP Analytics Cloud," plus KPIs on the home page. — [SAP Learning: Overview of SSCV2](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-sap-sales-cloud-version-2/getting-an-overview-about-sap-sales-cloud-version-2)
- SAP Community (SAP-authored blog listing available tables): embedded analytics "is based on the embedded edition of SAP Analytics Cloud (eSAC). It is not the full version." Admins build in a "Design Stories" work center and end users consume in an "Analysis" work center. Role-based access, calculated columns and filters are available. Standard data sources cover opportunity, account/contact, activity, service case, sales order/quote, lead, and product/pricing. — [SAP Community: Embedded Analytics for SSCV2](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-sap/embedded-analytics-for-sap-sales-amp-service-cloud-v2-list-of-available/ba-p/14284830)
  - Contradiction: the same post reportedly says V2 has no "native analytics," while an SAP Learning video describes basic analytics on the dashboard plus deeper analysis through stories. — [SAP Learning video: Analytics in SSCV2](https://learning.sap.com/videos/explaining-analytics-in-sap-sales-cloud-version-2-1)
- Pipeline and forecasting (SAP Learning): **Pipeline Manager** uses bubble and bar-line charts to track opportunities by close date, risk level and value. **Forecast Tracker** lets managers submit, simulate and override forecasts based on live pipeline data or predictive algorithms. A "Trends Analytics" module covers pipeline and process flow. — [SAP Learning: Trends Analytics](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-sap-sales-cloud-version-2/streamlining-pipeline-and-process-flow-with-trends-analytics); [SAP Learning: Overview](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-sap-sales-cloud-version-2/getting-an-overview-about-sap-sales-cloud-version-2)
- Standalone SAC: a community member says SAP's recommended architecture for analyzing V2 data outside the tenant uses **SAP Datasphere as the central data layer with SAP Integration Suite for event-driven replication**. V2 "does not expose OData endpoints," which blocks SAC's generic OData connector, so the author proposes a workaround. — [SAP Community (member): Connecting SSCV2 to standalone SAC](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-members/connecting-sap-sales-cloud-v2-to-a-standalone-sap-analytics-cloud-a/ba-p/14310439)
  - Conflict: a third-party connector vendor claims it connects to "SAP Sales Cloud V1 (C4C) & SAP Sales Cloud V2 via their respective OData APIs." SAP's own SSCV2 extension material refers to **REST** APIs. — [PutItForward (vendor)](https://www.putitforward.com/platform/connectors/crm-platforms/sap-sales-cloud-integration); [SAP Community: SSCV2 APIs from SAP Build Apps](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-sap/calling-sap-sales-and-service-cloud-v2-apis-from-sap-build-apps/ba-p/13964852)
- SAP Business Data Cloud combines SAC, Datasphere, BW modernization, SAP Databricks and embedded apps (partner whitepaper). No SSCV2-specific BDC data product was confirmed. — [All for One (partner) whitepaper](https://www.all-for-one.pl/en/whitepapers/sap-business-data-cloud/)
- Partner view (Spadoom, 2026): V2 reporting is "more structured" than Salesforce's, built on **pre-defined report types**, and is less flexible. — [Spadoom: SSCV2 limitations](https://www.spadoom.com/en/blog/sap-sales-cloud-v2-limitations-honest-assessment/)
- User reviews (product version not specified; many are V1-era): TrustRadius reviewers call reporting and analytics "not intuitive enough." — [TrustRadius: SAP Sales Cloud reviews](https://www.trustradius.com/products/sap-sales-cloud/reviews)

### Inferences
- Operational pipeline and forecast reporting works in-tenant. Cross-domain analytics (CRM plus S/4HANA margins and volumes) will likely need Datasphere/BDC plus Integration Suite pipelines, which adds BTP licensing and build effort. This matters for companies whose sales KPIs depend on ERP data (e.g., volumes, prices, margins by channel).
- Because analytics run on eSAC rather than full SAC, planning and predictive scenarios beyond the Forecast Tracker probably need standalone SAC licences.

### Gaps
- No SAP primary source found on an official SSCV2 to Datasphere/BDC connector or data product as of Oct 2026.
- No detail on forecast-model accuracy, or on whether forecasting supports product- and quantity-level (not only revenue) forecasts.

## 3. Integrations (S/4HANA cloud and on-premise/private, ECC, CPQ, Commerce, Emarsys/Engagement Cloud, Service Cloud, Microsoft 365, telephony)

### Takeaway
SAP ships **prepackaged Integration Suite (Cloud Integration) iFlows** for SSCV2 with S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, S/4HANA (on-premise/private) and ECC. They cover business partner and master data replication, external pricing and ATP for quotes, and quote and sales order replication. The details have conditions attached: ECC needs **6.0 EhP8** for bidirectional business partner replication, scope documents exclude some flows, and partners describe pricing conflicts (real-time versus scheduled replication). Outlook and Teams integration is native. CTI, CPQ, Emarsys and Commerce mostly rely on Integration Suite, middleware or partner work rather than turnkey connectors.

## 4. Configuration and extensibility

### Takeaway
SSCV2 offers in-app, no-code configuration (custom fields, page layouts, mashups, interaction types, Autoflow rules) and a **side-by-side** extension model on SAP BTP (SAP Build Apps/Code, CAP, Kyma/Cloud Foundry, Build Process Automation for approvals). Synchronous "External Hooks" and asynchronous events link the tenant to BTP microservices. V1's in-tenant ABAP-like SDK (PDI) is gone, so complex custom logic, custom business objects and custom approvals generally mean building and running BTP apps.

### Cited Findings
- SAP Learning: extension tiers are **in-app** (fields, layouts, mashups), **low-code/no-code** with SAP Build (formerly AppGyver), and **complex/side-by-side** extensions on SAP BTP or Kyma. — [SAP Learning: Overview of SSCV2](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-sap-sales-cloud-version-2/getting-an-overview-about-sap-sales-cloud-version-2); [SAP Learning: In-app extensibility, Interaction Types](https://learning.sap.com/courses/extending-sap-sales-cloud-and-sap-service-cloud-version-2-using-in-app-extensibility/introducing-interaction-types)
- **Autoflow** is the rule-based automation: "A rule defines what event will trigger the automation and what actions will follow," with no custom code. To call external logic, admins create an outbound communication system with the service URL, create an autoflow, set a condition and add an event notification that targets a BTP microservice. — [GitHub: SSv2 extensibility workshop (SAP employee)](https://github.com/jens-limbach/SSv2-extensibility-workshop)
- **Standard Events** fire after an entity is processed and saved (asynchronous). **External Hooks** are synchronous: "Because the business entity operation waits for a response from External Hooks, you have direct control over how processing proceeds." — [SAP Learning: Custom logic microservice with SAP Build Code](https://learning.sap.com/courses/extending-sap-sales-cloud-and-sap-service-cloud-version-2-using-sap-build-code/configuring-and-testing-a-custom-logic-microservice-in-sap-sales-cloud-and-sap-service-cloud-version-2); [SAP Community (member): Extending SSCV2 using CAP](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-members/extending-sap-sales-and-service-cloud-v2-using-cap/ba-p/14148780)
- **Custom approvals:** SAP recommends **SAP Build Process Automation (BPA)**. When a saved object meets a condition, V2 starts a BPA workflow with decision tables, for both standard and custom objects (SAP blog, ~Aug 2026). — [SAP Community: Custom approval workflows with SAP BPA](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-sap/a-beginner-s-guide-to-custom-approval-workflows-in-sap-sales-amp-service/ba-p/14459520)
- **Custom objects:** a community guide says you **cannot register entirely new business objects with their own list and detail pages in-app**, so BTP-hosted apps are used. A CAP example builds the backend on HANA to feed a custom object's front end. This may be outdated; see Gaps. — [SAP Community (member): Extending SSCV2 using CAP](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-members/extending-sap-sales-and-service-cloud-v2-using-cap/ba-p/14148780); [Spadoom: Custom CF apps on BTP (2026)](https://www.spadoom.com/en/blog/extend-sap-sales-cloud-v2-custom-cloud-foundry-apps-btp/)
- **APIs:** SSCV2 exposes REST APIs. SAP Build Apps can consume them once the API is set up as a BTP destination. — [SAP Community: Calling SSCV2 APIs from SAP Build Apps](https://community.sap.com/t5/crm-and-cx-blog-posts-by-sap/calling-sap-sales-and-service-cloud-v2-apis-from-sap-build-apps/ba-p/13964852)
- **No PDI in V2:** V2 has no PDI (V1 SDK) development, so tenants with many PDI solutions, mashups and custom integrations take longer to move. — [Knack Systems (partner)](https://www.knacksystems.com/blog/sap-sales-and-service-cloud-v1-to-v2-what-the-transition-really-involves-for-sales-and-customer-service-teams)
- **Joule Studio** (agent builder) is GA Q1 2026 per a partner, for custom agentic workflows. — [Spadoom Q1 2026](https://www.spadoom.com/en/blog/whats-new-sap-sales-cloud-v2-q1-2026/)
- Partner view: "complex approvals" remain a V2 limitation. — [Spadoom: SSCV2 limitations](https://www.spadoom.com/en/blog/sap-sales-cloud-v2-limitations-honest-assessment/)

### Inferences
- The architecture is cleaner than V1's (microservices, events, an upgrade-safe core; Gartner praised the "composable and microservice architecture"; see section 5). The cost moves to BTP: subscriptions (Integration Suite, BPA, Build, Kyma/CF, AI Core) and developer skills in CAP/Node/Java instead of ABAP-like PDI. Total cost of ownership should include these.
- Business rules that V1 customers coded in PDI (e.g., pricing validations, credit logic, mandatory-field logic tied to ERP) will need External Hooks plus BTP microservices. Each one adds a runtime dependency and latency.

### Gaps
- Whether SSCV2 added in-app custom business objects (with their own UI) in 2025–2026 releases could not be verified from primary release notes.
- No official limits found (number of custom fields, API rate limits, Autoflow quotas).
